# Metis

Welcome to the **Metis** project.

## Why Metis

In Greek mythology, Metis is the goddess of wisdom and good organization. This name perfectly symbolizes the app's goal: to help users maintain a clear and orderly view of what they own, so they don't forget anything and can better manage their daily lives.

## Description

Metis is a home inventory management app. It allows you to easily track the quantities of items in your cupboards, whether food or non-food. Thanks to a category system, you can organize your belongings in a simple and intuitive way, and see at a glance what you have available at home.
